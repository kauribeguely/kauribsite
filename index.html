About

where art meets tech

My passion for visual creation and technology began in childhood, drawing in MSPaint and building game levels.

This led me to a Computer Science degree (with my favourite paper being Computer Graphics and Animation). I've consistently pursued coding through side projects, achieving recognition in AR development with Meta Spark.

Currently, I specialize in front-end development, with a love for 3D web animations using Three.js, and I'm exploring web AR with Google's Mediapipe.
